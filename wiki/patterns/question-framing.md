# Question framing

## Failure mode
Dumping narrative and ending with “thoughts?” — the responder cannot tell which decision to make.

## Workaround
1. One-line headline with ticket/RFC/path
2. One paragraph of background
3. Clean A vs B with observable consequences
4. Targeted sub-questions
5. Out of scope / already decided
6. Optional prior-art pointer
